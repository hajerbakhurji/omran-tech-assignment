## Why Light Gauge Steel (LGS)
LGS speeds up construction and lowers costs; it insulates sound and resists wind, fire and mould.
- Fast to build
- Eco-friendly
- Light, easy to transport and install
- Easy to recycle
- High heat and sound insulation
- Far fewer errors than concrete
- Rust-proof, lasts 150+ years
- High earthquake and fire resistance
